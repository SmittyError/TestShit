Gorilla Info (Very Scientific)

  • Gorillas are the largest living primates on Earth.
  • There are two species, the eastern and the western gorilla, each with two subspecies. Harambe's kind, the western lowland gorilla, is critically endangered.
  • Gorillas share roughly 98% of their DNA with humans. Simon shares about 4% with a functioning conscience.
  • Adult males are called silverbacks because of the silver saddle of hair that grows on their backs as they mature.
  • Gorillas are mostly vegetarian. A big male can eat around 40 lb (18 kg) of plants in a single day.
  • They build a fresh nest every night out of leaves and branches. Five-star hotel behaviour.
  • Every gorilla has a unique nose print, a bit like a human fingerprint.
  • Gorillas get around by knuckle-walking on all fours.
  • A gorilla group is called a troop, and the dominant silverback leads it.
  • Gorillas have been recorded humming and "singing" while they eat good food.
  • A silverback's chest beat can be heard up to about a kilometre away.
  • Gorillas generally can't swim and avoid deep water.
  • Koko the gorilla learned more than 1,000 signs of a modified sign language.
